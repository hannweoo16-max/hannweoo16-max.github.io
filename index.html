What changes

From paper handovers to one job record

The trucks, crews and warehouse stay the same. What changes is how information moves between them.

Plan

JI typed in Word, sent on WhatsApp

Job record with service, addresses, CBM and insurance. JI PDF in one tap.

Prepare

Material list rewritten for every job

Copy from the Materials Master, CBM calculated, Pick All for the warehouse.

Pack & inspect

One supervisor numbers every box by hand

QR labels for every packer; loading order assigned separately. Photos filed under the job.

Receive & sign

Paper tally, re-counted twice

Tap-to-tally grid with damage photos. DO, tally sheet and packing list generated and signed.

Who it's for

Built for crews, not desks

Movers & relocation

Local, import, export and office moves with multi-day packing, several addresses and storage. Proof of condition before and after every job.

Delivery operators

Daily DOs, pre-delivered materials, photo proof and customer sign-off, without a separate app for each step.

Small 3PL warehouses

Store-in receiving against a packing list, tallies that work in any order, and a clear record of what came back.
