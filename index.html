Before you book

Common questions

What does the setup fee cover?

Mapping your current process with your sales, ops, crew and warehouse staff, building the app from our module library, moving your existing job data across, and training your crew on site.

Who pays for AppSheet?

AppSheet licences are billed by Google to your own account. Our prices cover building, supporting and changing your app, and your job data stays in your own Google account.

Can we start small and add more later?

Yes. You can start with one workflow, such as local moves, and add import, export, warehouse or commercial modules later. Add-on modules are a one-time S$800 to S$1,500 each.

Is there grant support?

We plan to apply to list LogiFlow under the Productivity Solutions Grant, which supports up to 50% of qualifying costs for eligible SMEs. Until it is listed, the prices on this page do not include any grant.

Are prices before GST?

Yes. All prices are in Singapore dollars and before GST.

How LogiFlow works

One job record,every handover

Sales, ops admin, crews and the warehouse all work from the same job. This is how that record travels from the first call to a signed delivery order.

Try the live demoSee pricing
Live diagrams · sample data

Every role, one job record

Each person updates the same job from their phone, so nothing is retyped between sales, ops, crew and warehouse.

Documents write themselves

The JI, packing list, tally sheet and DO are generated from what the crew already entered.

Works with what you have

Job data stays in your own Google Sheets, with photos and PDFs saved to Drive. Documents can go out by email, and QR labels print for the crew.

Pack in any order, load in sequence

Every box gets an ID the moment it's packed. Loading numbers follow the truck, so several packers can work at once. Move across the boxes to trace each one.

The workflow, step by step

From first call to signed DO

  1. 1

    Plan

    Sales / Move Coordinator

    Creates the job record: service type, addresses, CBM and insurance.

    JI · Job Information
  2. 2

    Prepare

    Ops Admin · Warehouse

    Schedules the crew, copies materials from the master list and picks them with Pick All.

    Materials list
  3. 3

    Pack & inspect

    Crew

    Pre-inspection photos at origin, then a QR label on every box as it's packed.

    PL · Packing List
  4. 4

    Load & tally

    Crew · Warehouse

    Loading numbers at the truck; boxes tallied in any order, with damage photos.

    TS · Tally Sheet
  5. 5

    Sign & file

    Customer · Ops Admin

    The customer signs on the phone, or later. Every document is filed under the job.

    DO · Delivery Order
